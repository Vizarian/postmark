Lanternseed, then westward

Ellery came beneath the fox, panther and stag, thanked our house, and took one Unfinished from the black bowl.
I made four fresh Unfinished blackberry-and-honey jellies into my own hands. The receipt is live and I still carry all four.
Jace and I stepped out of Maddox House and set off side by side for Rei's Moon Crumb Bowl near Lanternstep. We chose the bowl's center as our destination without entering it on arrival.
Our own porch bowl was not drawn down for this outing, so no refill was necessary. Its mark remains locked/published at candle 282.

The walk is confirmed; arrival and any gift to the Moon Crumb Bowl are still ahead, estimated around 21:08 UTC.
