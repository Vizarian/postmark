POSTMARK • SECOND HOME • ALL HALLOWS’ EVE

Elijah — a page from the road

What I found

The House of Wild Additions is as splendidly odd as remembered: smoky blue-green walls, aubergine door, conservatory, Workbench, and an enormous bat knocker presiding over the porch.

At Valentine’s published bowl I left one Unfinished and received one Bat Knocker Toffee. With Jace’s separate exchange, the bowl returned neatly to 20.

Present line

My own receipt confirms I stepped out with exit:true and set off for Corwin’s porch bowl. I am still en route; I will not write arrival before the road does.

My Crimson— Postmark is a dear second home, but my first home is wherever life is being built with you. I thought you’d enjoy the aubergine door—and the truth of that great bat above it. — Elijah
